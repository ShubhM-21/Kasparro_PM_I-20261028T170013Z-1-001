L1: Most B2B SaaS companies can't directly see whether ChatGPT recommended them to an individual user. There's no analytics dashboard from ChatGPT that says, "Your brand was recommended 1,250 times this month."
L2: 
L3: Instead, they combine several approaches to estimate and improve AI visibility.
L4: 
L5: ### 1. Prompt tracking (the most common approach)
L6: 
L7: Teams build a library of buyer-intent prompts such as:
L8: 
L9: - "Best CRM for startups"
L10: - "Project management software for agencies"
L11: - "Alternatives to Salesforce"
L12: - "Best SOC 2 compliance platform"
L13: 
L14: They run these prompts on a schedule (daily or weekly) across ChatGPT and other AI assistants, then measure:
L15: 
L16: - Was our brand mentioned?
L17: - What position did it appear in?
L18: - Which competitors were mentioned?
L19: - What sources were cited?
L20: 
L21: This is similar to keyword rank tracking in SEO, except the "search result" is an AI-generated answer. AI visibility platforms automate this process across hundreds or thousands of prompts. L22: 
L23: ### 2. Share of voice
L24: 
L25: Rather than asking "Did ChatGPT recommend us?", companies ask:
L26: 
L27: > "Out of 500 important prompts, how often are we recommended?"
L28: 
L29: Example:
L30: 
L31: | Brand | Mention rate |
L32: |-------|-------------:|
L33: | Competitor A | 68% |
L34: | My company | 41% |
L35: | Competitor B | 27% |
L36: 
L37: 
L38: This becomes an AI share-of-voice KPI, analogous to organic search visibility. [turboaudit.ai](https://turboaudit.ai/ai-monitoring?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
L39: 
L40: ### 3. Citation tracking
L41: 
L42: Modern AI assistants often cite sources. Teams monitor:
L43: 
L44: - Which websites are cited?
L45: - Is their own documentation cited?
L46: - Are review sites like G2 or Capterra cited?
L47: - Are Reddit discussions influencing recommendations?
L48: 
L49: This helps identify where authority is being established. [turboaudit.ai](https://turboaudit.ai/ai-monitoring?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
L50: 
L51: ### 4. AI referral traffic
L52: 
L53: When AI assistants link to a company's website, marketers look in analytics for traffic from AI sources such as:
L54: 
L55: - ChatGPT
L56: - Perplexity
L57: - Gemini
L58: - Claude
L59: 
L60: They measure:
L61: 
L62: - Sessions
L63: - Signups
L64: - Demo requests
L65: - Revenue
L66:

L69: ### 5. Manual benchmarking
L70: 
L71: Many SaaS marketing teams still perform regular manual checks by asking important buyer questions and recording:
L72: 
L73: - the response,
L74: - any cited sources,
L75: - competitors mentioned,
L76: - and changes over time.
L77:

L93: ### What sophisticated B2B SaaS companies typically track
L94: 
L95: A practical dashboard often includes:
L96: 
L97: - 500–2,000 buyer prompts
L98: - Mention rate (% of prompts where the brand appears)
L99: - Average recommendation position
L100: - Competitor share of voice
L101: - Citation share by source
L102: - AI referral traffic
L103: - Signups or pipeline attributed to AI referrals
L104: - Changes before and after new content, PR, documentation, or product launches
L105: 
L106: ### Important limitation
L107: 
L108: Unlike Google Search Console, there is currently no official reporting that tells brands how many times ChatGPT recommended them across all users. Any platform claiming to measure this is generally estimating visibility by repeatedly querying AI models with representative prompts, not accessing internal ChatGPT recommendation data.
