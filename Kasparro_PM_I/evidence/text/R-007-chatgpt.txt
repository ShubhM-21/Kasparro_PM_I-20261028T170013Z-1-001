L1: Kasparro's **Four Module Audit Score** appears to be a proprietary assessment framework that measures **how visible and "recommendable" a brand is to AI assistants** (such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and Copilot). According to Kasparro's published methodology, the audit produces scores across four areas rather than relying on traditional SEO or ad metrics. [kasparro.com](https://kasparro.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
L2: 
L3: The four modules are:
L4: 
L5: 1. **Search Visibility**
L6:    - Measures how often your brand appears in high-intent AI conversations.
L7:    - Looks at metrics such as:
L8:      - Inclusion rate
L9:      - Comparative recall
L10:      - Coverage across supported AI systems
L11:    - This answers questions like: *"When someone asks an AI for the best protein powder, does my brand get mentioned?"* [kasparro.com](https://kasparro.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
L12: 
L13: 2. **Trust Signals**
L14:    - Evaluates whether AI systems can find evidence that supports recommending your brand.
L15:    - Includes factors such as:
L16:      - Third-party citations
L17:      - Verifiable claims
L18:      - Source quality
L19:      - Claim alignment
L20:    - AI models generally favor information they can corroborate from reliable sources. [kasparro.com](https://kasparro.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
L21: 
L22: 3. **Content Quality**
L23:    - Reviews how well your website, catalog, and content are structured for language models.
L24:    - Metrics mentioned include:
L25:      - Catalog coverage
L26:      - Attribute density
L27:      - Embedding consistency ("embedding drift")
L28:    - The emphasis is on structured, complete product information rather than marketing copy alone. [kasparro.com](https://kasparro.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
L29: 
L30: 4. **Transaction Readiness**
L31:    - Measures whether AI agents can easily complete or facilitate a purchase.
L32:    - Factors include:
L33:      - Structured data (schema)
L34:      - Price availability
L35:      - Fulfillment information
L36:      - Site latency
L37:      - Agent-ready product listings
L38:    - The goal is to make products easy for AI systems to understand and act on. [kasparro.com](https://kasparro.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
L39: 
L40: ### Is there one overall score?
L41: 
L42: The site suggests these four modules roll up into an overall diagnostic score (examples like **82/100** are shown), but Kasparro does **not publicly document** the exact weighting or mathematical formula used to combine them. It appears to be a proprietary scoring model rather than an industry standard. [kasparro.com](https://kasparro.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
L43: 
L44: ### What the score is trying to predict
L45: 
L46: Unlike traditional SEO metrics that focus on Google rankings, Kasparro's score is intended to estimate your brand's readiness for **AI-mediated commerce**—specifically:
L47: - whether AI assistants mention your brand,
L48: - whether they trust it enough to recommend it,
L49: - whether they understand your products,
L50: - and whether they can easily direct or complete a purchase. [kasparro.com](https://kasparro.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
L51: 
L52: In short, the Four Module Audit Score is an **AI visibility and recommendation readiness score**, built around those four dimensions. The exact scoring algorithm is not publicly disclosed, so while the framework is described, the internal calculation remains proprietary.
