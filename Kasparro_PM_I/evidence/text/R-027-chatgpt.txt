L16: ### What these tools typically measure
L17: 
L18: Unlike traditional SEO rank trackers, these platforms evaluate how your brand appears inside AI-generated answers. Common metrics include:
L19: 
L20: - **Brand visibility**: How often your brand appears in responses to a defined prompt set.
L21: - **Share of voice (SoV)**: Your percentage of brand mentions compared with competitors.
L22: - **Citation analysis**: Which websites or sources AI models cite when mentioning your brand.
L23: - **Mention position**: Whether you're the first recommendation or further down the answer.
L24: - **Sentiment**: Whether AI describes your brand positively, neutrally, or negatively.
L25: - **Competitor benchmarking**: Which prompts competitors win and where they outrank you.
L26: - **Historical trends**: Changes in visibility over days, weeks, or months. [higoodie.com](https://higoodie.com/features/ai-visibility-monitoring/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)[peec.ai](https://peec.ai/ai-instructions?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

L42: ### Things to watch for
L43: 
L44: AI search is probabilistic: the same prompt can produce different answers over time. Because of this, stronger tools:
L45: - run prompts repeatedly rather than once,
L46: - report trends instead of one-off rankings,
L47: - allow custom prompt sets that reflect your customers' real questions. Researchers have noted that repeated measurements are essential for reliable AI visibility metrics. [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.08919?utm_source=chatgpt.com)[arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07585?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

L49: ### Which tool fits which use case?
L50: 
L51: - **Large enterprise:** Profound or Goodie.
L52: - **Marketing teams focused on reporting and dashboards:** Peec AI.
L53: - **Content optimization plus AI visibility:** AthenaHQ.
L54: - **Smaller businesses or agencies:** Otterly.AI.
L55: - **Already using an SEO suite:** Semrush or Ahrefs can be a lower-friction addition.
L56: 
L57: If your goal is measuring **brand appearance across AI search engines**, the dedicated AI visibility platforms (Profound, Peec AI, Goodie, AthenaHQ, and Otterly.AI) generally provide deeper analytics than traditional SEO tools, which have only recently added AI visibility features.
