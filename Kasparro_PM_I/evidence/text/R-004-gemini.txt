L5: If you hit a wall after two weeks, here is what is likely happening and what you should do next:
L6: 
L7: ### 1. Diagnose the Root Cause
L8: 
L9: * **The "Entity Mismatch" Problem:** AI models recognize brands as distinct "entities".[crecerank.com](https://crecerank.com/what-happens-if-my-ai-visibility-is-0-crecerank-guide/#:~:text=If%20your%20indicator%20in%20the%20GEO%20software,prompt.%20The%20prompt%20you%20use%20to%20measure) If the name, product variations, or brand terms configured in your tracking tool don't precisely match how LLMs map your business across the web, the tool will register flat or zero visibility.[crecerank.com](https://crecerank.com/what-happens-if-my-ai-visibility-is-0-crecerank-guide/#:~:text=Its%20sole%20objective%20is%20to%20deliver%20the,its%20conceptual%20map%2C%20the%20measurement%20will%20fail.)
L10: * **The Crawling Lag:** Unlike Google, which can index changes relatively quickly, AI bots (like GPTBot, ClaudeBot, or PerplexityBot) or the third-party sources they rely on may not have re-visited your updated pages or off-site mentions yet.
L11: * **Prompt Over-Broadening:** If the queries or prompts you are tracking are too generic (e.g., "best software"), your brand will get drowned out by enterprise giants.[crecerank.com](https://crecerank.com/what-happens-if-my-ai-visibility-is-0-crecerank-guide/#:~:text=Optimize%20the%20prompts%3A%20If%20your%20prompts%20are,over%20the%20typical%20blue%20links%20from%20Google.) AI tracking requires specific, niche-focused query mapping.[crecerank.com](https://crecerank.com/what-happens-if-my-ai-visibility-is-0-crecerank-guide/#:~:text=Optimize%20the%20prompts%3A%20If%20your%20prompts%20are,over%20the%20typical%20blue%20links%20from%20Google.)
L12: 
L13: ### 2. Actionable Steps to Take
L14: 
L15: * **Check Your Technical Access:** Ensure your `robots.txt` file isn't accidentally blocking AI crawlers.[crecerank.com](https://crecerank.com/what-happens-if-my-ai-visibility-is-0-crecerank-guide/#:~:text=No%2C%20not%20at%20all.%20AIs%20do%20not,%28like%20GPTBot%20or%20ClaudeBot%29%20from%20accessing%20it.) Many sites block them by default, which makes AI visibility impossible.
L16: * **Shift from Keywords to "Answer-First" Content:** AI engines don't look for keyword stuffing; they look for direct, concise answers.[www.frase.io](https://www.frase.io/blog/ai-visibility#:~:text=Content%20Optimization%20Strategies%20*%20Adopt%20answer-first%20content,point%20should%20link%20to%20its%20primary%20source.) Restructure your high-priority pages to answer primary user questions within the first 40–60 words.[www.frase.io](https://www.frase.io/blog/ai-visibility#:~:text=Content%20Optimization%20Strategies%20*%20Adopt%20answer-first%20content,point%20should%20link%20to%20its%20primary%20source.)
L17: * **Boost Off-Site Citations (Digital PR & Forums):** AI models heavily weight consensus from third-party ecosystems (Reddit, specialized forums, industry blogs, and review sites).[crecerank.com](https://crecerank.com/what-happens-if-my-ai-visibility-is-0-crecerank-guide/#:~:text=If%20your%20website%20is%20new%20or%20if,to%20validate%20and%20recommend%20your%20brand%20safely.) If your website alone is optimized, but the wider web isn't talking about you in a structured way, the AI won't recommend you.
L18: * **Implement Strict Structured Data:** Make sure you are utilizing Schema markup (FAQPage, Organization, Product) so AI crawlers can cleanly parse your information.[www.frase.io](https://www.frase.io/blog/ai-visibility#:~:text=You%20also%20need%20to%20ensure%20AI%20engines,more%20effectively.%20*%20Ensure%20fast%20page%20loads.)
L19: 
L20: ### 3. Managing Expectations
L21: 
L22: A 14-day trial or sprint is usually enough to audit your baseline and make technical fixes, but building genuine AI authority takes consistent, cumulative effort. Treat the first two weeks as a diagnostic phase to see where the disconnect lies between your brand and the AI's knowledge graph.
