L14: Key Metrics These Tools Measure
L15: 
L16: - **Mention Frequency:** How often a brand is named in AI-generated responses.
L17: - **Citation Share:** The percentage of cited references that point to your website.
L18: - **Share of Voice (SoV):** A brand's proportional presence in AI answers compared to competitors.
L19: - **Average Positioning:** Where a brand appears in a list of recommendations.
L20: - **Sentiment Analysis:** Whether the brand mention is positive, neutral, or negative.[](https://business.adobe.com/blog/track-brand-mentions-in-ai-search) [[1]](https://business.adobe.com/blog/track-brand-mentions-in-ai-search)[[2]](https://business.adobe.com/blog/improve-brand-visibility-in-ai-search-engines)[[3]](https://roirevolution.com/blog/ai-search-optimization-and-brand-authority/)[[4]](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/ai-search-analytics-tools)[[5]](https://llmpulse.ai/blog/best-se-ranking-ai-visibility-alternatives/)

L22: What to Look for in AI Visibility Tools
L23: 
L24: - **LLM Coverage:** Tracking across multiple engines (e.g., Gemini, Claude, Perplexity).
L25: - **Prompt Customization:** Ability to use specific industry queries to test visibility.
L26: - **Actionability:** Providing steps to improve, not just reporting gaps.[](https://www.capterra.com/resources/how-to-choose-ai-search-visibility-tools/) [[1]](https://wpengine.com/blog/ai-visibility-tracking-tools/)[[2]](https://www.webfx.com/blog/ai/ai-brand-visibility-traffic/)
