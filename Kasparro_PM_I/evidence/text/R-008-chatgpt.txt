L100: ### Are they direct competitors?
L101: 
L102: Only partially.
L103: 
L104: Kasparro's publicly described offering is centered on an **assessment framework** with actionable scores across four dimensions. Otterly includes an audit feature but is positioned as a broader operational platform for monitoring, analytics, and optimization over time. If your team needs ongoing visibility tracking and reporting, Otterly offers a more comprehensive operational toolkit. If the immediate goal is to diagnose AI discoverability and prioritize improvements, Kasparro's audit-first approach is more narrowly focused.
