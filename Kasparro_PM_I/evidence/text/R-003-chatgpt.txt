L1: If your primary goal is **tracking how Claude recommends B2B software brands**, I would lean toward **Profound** over Kasparro.
L2: 
L3: Here's why:
L4: 
L5: | Capability | Profound | Kasparro |
L6: |---|---|---|
L7: | Claude visibility tracking | ✅ Strong | ⚠️ Not its primary focus |
L8: | B2B SaaS prompt tracking | ✅ Yes | ❌ Primarily retail/CPG |
L9: | Competitor comparisons | ✅ Extensive | Limited for SaaS |
L10: | Prompt-level analytics | ✅ Yes | Focuses on shopping queries |
L11: | Enterprise AI search monitoring | ✅ ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, etc. | More focused on consumer purchase recommendations |
L12: 
L13: 
L14: Profound was built around measuring AI search visibility across multiple models—including Claude—and has features like:
L15: - prompt-level tracking,
L16: - citation monitoring,
L17: - competitive visibility,
L18: - trend reporting,
L19: - and analysis of which prompts your competitors win. [www.tryprofound.com](https://www.tryprofound.com/ai-instructions?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
L20: 
L21: Kasparro (assuming you mean the AI visibility platform rather than another product with a similar name) is aimed much more at **retail and CPG brands**. Its metrics revolve around AI shopping recommendations, "Brand Inclusion Rate," "Share of Invisible Shelf," and agentic commerce rather than B2B software evaluation. [getkasper.ai](https://getkasper.ai/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
L22: 
L23: One nuance, though: if you're specifically interested in **recommendation probability** ("Does Claude recommend Salesforce or HubSpot more often?") rather than **visibility/citations**, neither platform may be ideal on its own.
L24: 
L25: Several newer tools are trying to measure recommendation distributions rather than just mentions, because recommendation and visibility are different metrics. For example, some platforms repeatedly run buyer-style prompts against Claude to estimate recommendation frequency across many prompt variations. [genezio.com](https://genezio.com/blog/genezio-vs-profound/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
L26: 
L27: So I'd break it down like this:
L28: 
L29: - **For enterprise GEO/AI visibility:** Profound is the stronger choice.
L30: - **For tracking Claude's B2B SaaS recommendations over time:** Profound is still likely the better fit today, but supplementing it with your own recurring prompt testing (or a recommendation-focused platform) will give a more accurate picture.
L31: - **For retail/e-commerce brands:** Kasparro makes more sense.
L32: 
L33: If you're doing this for a SaaS company, I'd also ask: are you trying to measure:
L34: 1. **share of mentions** (visibility),
L35: 2. **#1 recommendation rate** (who Claude picks first),
L36: 3. **citations/sources**, or
L37: 4. **buyer-journey prompts** (e.g. "best CRM for startups" vs. "HubSpot alternatives")?
L38: 
L39: The best tool depends on which of those is your KPI.
