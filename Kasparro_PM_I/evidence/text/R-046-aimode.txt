L14: 2. Internal Tracking Tools (High Effort, Hidden Costs)
L15: 
L16: Building an internal tool is generally only advised for companies with highly specialized needs or massive, enterprise-scale data requirements.
L17: 
L18: - **High Development Cost:** Building a tool to parse natural language summaries from different LLMs (Gemini, GPT) requires specialized engineering time.
L19: - **Maintenance Nightmare:** AI models change constantly; ensuring your parser still works is a perpetual, costly task.[](https://trustmary.com/ai-visibility/best-ai-search-visibility-tools/) [[1]](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/deanhunt_i-asked-ai-to-provide-a-morning-agenda-activity-7483905896662769664-7nhE)[[2]](https://smartdev.com/cost-roi-of-ai-development-services-ai-software-development-and-custom-ai-development-for-global-enterprises/)
L20: - **Data Reliability:** In-house scripts may fail to capture the nuanced "citation drift," where your brand appears and disappears from AI answers weekly.[](https://trustmary.com/ai-visibility/best-ai-search-visibility-tools/)
