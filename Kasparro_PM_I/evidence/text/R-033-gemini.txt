L7: ### 1. Traditional SEO: The Foundation of Trust
L8: 
L9: Traditional SEO focuses on crawlability, site speed, technical health, and domain authority (primarily driven by backlinks).[www.margen.net](https://www.margen.net/do-i-need-both-ai-seo-and-traditional-seo/#:~:text=They%20share%20the%20same%20foundations%20and%20target,technical%20health%2C%20quality%20content%2C%20and%20genuine%20authority.)
L10: 
L11: * **What it does for AI:** AI models do not pull information from random websites; they rely on retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), meaning they search the live web for trusted sources. If your technical SEO is poor, search bots cannot crawl your site efficiently, meaning AI search layers (like Perplexity or Google AI Overviews) will never even index your content. Furthermore, high domain authority and quality backlinks act as trust signals that tell an AI model your brand is safe to cite.[www.margen.net](https://www.margen.net/geo-vs-seo/#:~:text=Both%20systems%20reward%20clear%20HTML%20structure%20%E2%80%94,most%20powerful%20proxy%20for%20credibility%20in%20SEO.)
L12: * **Where it falls short:** Traditional SEO is designed to win *clicks* in a list of links.[www.entlify.com](https://www.entlify.com/blog/geo-vs-seo#:~:text=The%20key%20difference%20between%20GEO%20vs.%20SEO,Overviews%20are%20reshaping%20how%20people%20find%20information.) An article can rank #1 organically through keyword stuffing or long-winded introductions, but still get completely ignored by an AI engine because the text is too difficult for a language model to neatly parse and summarize.
L13: 
L14: ### 2. Dedicated AI Optimization (GEO): The Mechanism for Citations
L15: 
L16: Dedicated AI optimization (or Generative Engine Optimization) focuses on how language models ingest and synthesize text.
L17: 
L18: * **What it does for AI:** GEO reshapes content so it is optimized for *extraction*. Research from institutions like Princeton and Georgia Tech demonstrates that tactics specific to GEO—such as adding clear statistical data, direct definitions, quotable expert statements, and structured markup (like FAQs)—can improve AI visibility by **30% to 40%** without changing a page's backlink profile.[www.margen.net](https://www.margen.net/geo-vs-seo/#:~:text=The%20gap%20between%20those%20two%20lists%20is,visibility%20by%2030%E2%80%9340%25%20based%20on%20published%20research.)
L19: * **Where it falls short:** Dedicated AI optimization tools or tactics cannot create citations out of thin air. If a brand has zero topical authority, no technical footprint, and no external validation (backlinks), optimizing text formatting won't convince an AI model to trust it.

L37: 
L38: 1.  **Traditional SEO gets you to the table:** It ensures your site is indexed, fast, crawlable, and authoritative enough for AI crawlers to discover.[medium.com](https://medium.com/marketing-102/the-difference-between-traditional-seo-and-generative-engine-optimization-geo-b96b79399679#:~:text=Here%20is%20how%20they%20support%20each%20other%3A,framing%20so%20AI%20feels%20safe%20citing%20you.)
L39: 2.  **AI optimization seals the deal:** Once the AI bot arrives at your page, GEO formatting (direct answers, structured tables, clean data points, and explicit entity references) ensures the model understands your content well enough to safely quote and cite you.[medium.com](https://medium.com/marketing-102/the-difference-between-traditional-seo-and-generative-engine-optimization-geo-b96b79399679#:~:text=For%20example%2C%20instead%20of%20%E2%80%9CBenefits%20of%20GEO%2C%E2%80%9D,Use%20schema%20markup%20to%20make%20meaning%20explicit.)
