L1: The cost of enterprise **AI visibility, audit, and governance platforms** varies widely depending on whether you are tracking your brand’s presence in external AI search engines (Generative Engine Optimization/GEO) or auditing internal enterprise LLMs, agents, and data usage.[trussed.ai](https://trussed.ai/resources/enterprise-ai-governance-costs#:~:text=Dedicated%20governance%20ownership%2C%20commercial%20tooling%2C%20multiple%20frameworks%2C,and%20a%20single-market%20SaaS%20face%20different%20bills%29%3B)
L2: 
L3: Because features, deployment requirements, and tracking scales differ, pricing typically falls into distinct market segments:
L4: 
L5: ---
L6: 
L7: ### 1. External AI Visibility & Brand Audit (GEO)
L8: 
L9: *Tools that track how your brand appears across AI search engines, chatbots, and summaries (e.g., ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews).*
L10: 
L11: * **Entry/Growth Tiers:** **$30 to $300 per month**
L12:   *   * *Best for:* Mid-market brands, individual marketing teams, or agencies tracking a limited set of prompts and domains.
L13:   * *Examples:* Otterly, Semrush (AI search add-on packs), and specialized tracking tools.[siftly.ai](https://siftly.ai/blog/ai-visibility-tools-pricing-comparison#:~:text=Profound%2C%20~%24499%2Fmo%2C%20Enterprise%2C%20Limited.%20*Indicative%20only%20%E2%80%94,Factors%20that%20push%20price%20as%20you%20scale.)
L14: * **Enterprise Tiers:** **$500 to $2,000+ per month** (or customized annual contracts)
L15:   *   * *Best for:* Large brands requiring deep historical tracking, thousands of tracked prompts, multi-location insights, and robust API integrations.[siftly.ai](https://siftly.ai/blog/ai-visibility-tools-pricing-comparison#:~:text=Growth%20teams%3A%20Siftly%20for%20monitoring%20plus%20the,enterprise%20use%20cases%29%2C%20and%20historical%20data%20depth.)
L16:   * *Examples:* Advanced enterprise variants of visibility platforms like Profound.[siftly.ai](https://siftly.ai/blog/ai-visibility-tools-pricing-comparison#:~:text=Profound%2C%20~%24499%2Fmo%2C%20Enterprise%2C%20Limited.%20*Indicative%20only%20%E2%80%94,Factors%20that%20push%20price%20as%20you%20scale.)
L17: 
L18: ---
L19: 
L20: ### 2. Internal AI Governance, Compliance, & Audit Platforms
L21: 
L22: *Platforms that audit internal LLM usage, monitor agent reasoning chains, enforce guardrails, and manage compliance (e.g., EU AI Act, SOC 2, HIPAA).*
L23: 
L24: * **Small to Mid-Market Organizations:** **$73,000 to $150,000+ per year** (or tool-specific plans starting around **$799 to $1,000+ per month**)[trussed.ai](https://trussed.ai/resources/enterprise-ai-governance-costs#:~:text=How%20much%20does%20AI%20governance%20cost%20by,internal%20hours.%20Mid-size%3A%20roughly%20%24150K%20to%20%24350K%2Fyear.)
L25:   *   * *Best for:* Smaller companies managing initial AI deployments, lightweight auditing tooling, and baseline compliance policies.[trussed.ai](https://trussed.ai/resources/enterprise-ai-governance-costs#:~:text=How%20much%20does%20AI%20governance%20cost%20by,internal%20hours.%20Mid-size%3A%20roughly%20%24150K%20to%20%24350K%2Fyear.)
L26: * **Large Enterprises:** **$350,000 to $650,000+ per year[trussed.ai](https://trussed.ai/resources/enterprise-ai-governance-costs#:~:text=Dedicated%20governance%20ownership%2C%20commercial%20tooling%2C%20multiple%20frameworks%2C,and%20a%20single-market%20SaaS%20face%20different%20bills%29%3B)**
L27:   *   * *Best for:* Fortune 500 companies, highly regulated sectors (finance, healthcare), or massive multi-system deployments requiring continuous monitoring, multi-framework mapping, dedicated support, and on-premise/single-tenant VPC setups.[trussed.ai](https://trussed.ai/resources/enterprise-ai-governance-costs#:~:text=Enterprise%20AI%20governance%20costs%20range%20from%20roughly,and%20whether%20governance%20is%20manual%20or%20automated.)
L28: 
L29: ---
L30: 
L31: ### 3. AI Observability & Token/Cost Auditing
L32: 
L33: *Tools focused on tracking LLM token consumption, tracing agent workflows, and debugging output accuracy.*
L34: 
L35: * **Consumption or Seat-Based Models:**
L36:   *   * **Per-Seat / Per-User:** Typically **$8 to $60 per user/month** for workspace access and basic platform governance.
L37:   * **Ingestion / Token Volume Pricing:** Scaled tiered pricing based on gigabytes of log ingestion or millions of tokens traced. Many open-source or developer-first tools (like Langfuse, Arize Phoenix, or OpenObserve) offer generous free tiers or self-hosted options, scaling into custom enterprise contracts for high-volume production tracking.[www.braintrust.dev](https://www.braintrust.dev/articles/best-ai-observability-tools-2026#:~:text=Captures%20comprehensive%20agent%20traces%20with%20automated%20evaluation%2C,agent%20traces%2C%20real-time%20guardrails%2C%20and%20compliance%20monitoring.)
L38:

L41: ### Core Cost Drivers to Watch For
L42: 
L43: When budgeting for an enterprise AI audit or visibility platform, vendors usually anchor their pricing to a few specific variables:
L44: 
L45: 1.  **Scope of Coverage:** Whether you are tracking public brand mentions or securing hundreds of internal corporate AI agents and data connectors.
L46: 2.  **Data Volume & Retention:** Number of prompts tracked daily, API calls monitored, or length of audit log retention required for compliance.[www.semrush.com](https://www.semrush.com/pricing/seo-ai-search/#:~:text=SEO%3A%205%20websites%20to%20monitor.%20500%20keywords,websites.%20248.17%20dollars%20monthly.%20%24248.17%2Fmo.%20billed%20annually.)
L47: 3.  **Deployment Model:** Standard multi-tenant cloud vs. dedicated VPC, single-tenant, or on-premise installation (which heavily drives up enterprise implementation fees).[trussed.ai](https://trussed.ai/resources/enterprise-ai-governance-costs#:~:text=Year-one%20ISO%2042001%20pursuit%20averages%20~%2473K%20including,audit%20involvement.%20What%20drives%20AI%20governance%20costs%3F)
L48: 
L49: Are you looking to audit **external brand visibility** in AI answers, or do you need to govern and monitor **internal enterprise AI models and agents**?
