L3: Its core audit evaluates a brand's standing across **four key dimensions of AI commerce and visibility**:[getmereferred.com](https://getmereferred.com/in/job-listing/content-and-community-associate-kasparro-ai-inkabengaluru-to-3-years-experience-a83a9c86-b25b-4b5f-9fc2-4adfaf9fb550#:~:text=Our%20core%20product%20is%20a%20multi-module%20audit,systems%20see%20and%20represent%20them%20before%20those)
L4: 
L5: 1.  **Search Visibility:** How frequently and prominently a brand appears when users query AI assistants for relevant product or service categories (essentially, how well the brand shows up on the "AI shelf").
L6: 2.  **Trust Signals (E-E-A-T & Authority):** How trustworthy the brand appears to LLMs, evaluated through off-site footprints like Reddit discussions, third-party reviews, Wikipedia, citations, and authority sources.
L7: 3.  **Content Quality:** Whether the underlying digital content structure matches what AI engines parse, synthesize, and extract when building answers for prospective buyers.
L8: 4.  **Transaction Readiness:** How seamlessly an AI engine can guide a user from discovery to evaluation or conversion (whether the brand provides clear pathways, structured data, and context for commercial intent).
L9: 
L10: Through these metrics, the Kasparro audit provides quantitative scores, specific content/technical findings, and actionable recommendations to help brands ensure they are actively recommended during AI-driven buyer research.
